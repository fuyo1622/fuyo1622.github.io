Coverage Gap Analysis for In-Cabin Object Detection

  • Used pose estimates to find sparse driver-position and orientation regions in an in-cabin dataset.
  • Generated targeted samples with camera-controlled video synthesis and LoRA, validated their features, and replaced samples while keeping the dataset size fixed.
